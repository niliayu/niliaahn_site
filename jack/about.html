About

Jack wakes up in the middle of a vast hedge maze with no idea how he got there, and a field of broken pumpkins around him. As he searches for a way out, he encounters one strange creature after another, some friendlier than others. With each step through the maze, Jack learns more about himself and begins to sense a connection to the mysteroius castle looming beyond the hedges. But his journey is only just beginning, and the secrets waiting inside the castle go deeper than he could ever imagine.

Jack the Lantern Boy is my original webcomic about a little pumpkin boy named Jack.
